NP Membership

NP Membership is available for nonprofit organizations at no cost. Members receive networking opportunities, chamber communications, and access to community events.

Bronze Membership

Bronze members receive a business directory listing, networking opportunities, and invitations to chamber events.

Silver Membership

Silver members receive all Bronze benefits plus event discounts, training opportunities, and additional business promotion.

Gold Membership

Gold members receive all Silver benefits plus homepage spotlight advertising, premium marketing opportunities, and VIP event access.
